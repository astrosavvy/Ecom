# Younoya Domain Principles & Philosophy

## 1. Brand Experience Philosophy
Younoya is designed as a luxury editorial object rather than a generic catalogue. The visitor discovers emotional meaning before seeing prices or product lists.

**Intended Emotional Progression**:
`Curiosity -> Immersion -> Recognition -> Intention -> Purchase`

- **The First Screen**: Intentionally minimal. Video and opening lines carry the impression:
  - *"A gift should feel inevitable."*
  - *"Four intentions. One continuous journey."*
  - *"The object is beautiful. The meaning is yours."*
- No generic promotional popups, no discount badges, and no aggressive carousels.

## 2. The Four Sacred Gift Intentions
All offerings and keepsakes map into 4 narrative emotional chapters:
1. **Love & Connection**: Connection • Affection • Emotional Presence
2. **Confidence & Personal Power**: Confidence • Courage • Self-Expression
3. **Vitality & Inner Balance**: Energy • Calm • Clarity • Grounding
4. **Wealth & Prosperity**: Financial Intention • Stability • Growth Mindset

## 3. Astrology & Personalization Guidance Concept
- **Inputs**: Name (numerology), Date of Birth, Time of Birth, Place of Birth.
- **Engine**: Ephemeris calculation of Moon Sign (Rashi), Nakshatra, and current Vimshottari Antardasha.
- **Mapping**: `12 Moon Signs × 9 Antardashas -> 4 Gift Intentions`.
- **Placement**: This is a dedicated journey (`/personalise`), NOT an invasive form on the homepage hero.
