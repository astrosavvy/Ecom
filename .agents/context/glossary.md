## 2. Typography Hierarchy

### Display Font
- **Typeface**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Regular & Italic), `Cinzel` (sparingly for brand identity mark / sanctum roman numerals).
- **Application**: Main headlines, editorial quotes, intention chapter titles, product emotional titles.
- **Rule**: Avoid clunky heavy all-caps blocks; pair Roman and Italic weights for Cartier-grade editorial rhythm (e.g. *Objects of Affection* / *for every chapter.*).

### Body & Interface Font
- **Typeface**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Inter` (weights: 400, 500, 600, 700).
- **Application**: Product descriptions, toolkit questions, navigation links, buttons, cart drawer, checkout, specs.
- **Rule**: High legibility, clean letter spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em` for body; `0.14em` to `0.20em` for small uppercase UI labels).

### Logo & Brand Mark
- **Rule**: Minimal, typographic, luxury-inspired. Optional subtle celestial orbit or star spark mark. Never use loud or generic clip-art astrological glyphs.

## 3. Product Photography & Image Styles

1. **Clean Commerce Images**:
   - Soft-neutral/warm-ivory backgrounds showcasing product details, materials, scale, craftsmanship, and unboxing components.
2. **Editorial Lifestyle Images**:
   - Meaningful lifestyle settings: intimate bedside tables, festive brass trays, gift unboxing moments, reading nooks.
3. **Symbolic Visuals**:
   - Soft celestial illumination, handwritten intention scrolls, rich velvet and satin textures, raw crystals, brass vessels, constellation arcs, and wax seals.

## 4. Interaction & Motion Nomenclature

- **Curated Gift Hamper**: The core Younoya offering — a luxury tray or basket containing 2–3 complementary products (candle, crystal in brass urn, sacred gold keepsake).
- **Scroll-Scrubbed Film**: Video element whose playback position (`video.currentTime`) is tied strictly to viewport scroll progression (`0.0` to `1.0`).
- **3D Perspective Cylinder**: Rotating stage (`transform-style: preserve-3d`) showcasing authentic keepsakes in an orbital carousel.
- **Scroll-to-Expand Storefront**: Smooth transition where the floating browser window expands from a compact preview into the full live website viewport.
- **Signature Foot-Glow Button**: Multi-stop radial gold gradient pooled at the base of buttons, softly illuminating upon interaction.
